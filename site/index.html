Red Sky
Interactive

A San Francisco interactive studio. These are its demo discs, desktop apps and Shockwave pieces, restored so they play in a web browser again.

Everything here runs the original 1990s files. The Director movies play in DirPlayer, an open-source Director player, and the Beatnik music plays in NeoBAE, the open-source Beatnik engine. Fixes and replaced assets are listed on each work's page.

Opened from a downloaded copy? Start it with "Start Red Sky 98.command"; browsers block pages opened as files.
