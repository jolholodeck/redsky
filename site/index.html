Notes

How this was rebuilt

Controls

  • Ctrl-E Entertainment, Ctrl-W Work, Ctrl-X Exit, as on the CD.
  • Enter returns from a piece to the Work menu when you reached it through the CD.
  • Space skips the Entertainment intro screens.
  • Pieces opened directly from this page can't return to the menu; use the browser's back button.

Known gaps

  • Mia Hamm draws its 2-bit graphics as blank boxes.
  • QuickTime clips don't play; pieces fall back to the CD's own non-QuickTime paths.
  • The Speaker and Exit modules are untested.
  • The CD's hardware checks (256 colors, CPU and audio-latency tests) are bypassed.
