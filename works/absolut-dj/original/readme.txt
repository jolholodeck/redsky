If you insert the Absolut DJ CD-ROM and the start-up files do not
automatically open your Internet browser, please follow these steps:

1) Open your Internet browser
2) Choose "File Open"
3) Select from one of the files below:

For PC users, open the "Absolut_DJ.htm" file

For Mac users, open either "my_browser_is_Netscape.htm" or
"my_browser_is_Microsoft.htm."
