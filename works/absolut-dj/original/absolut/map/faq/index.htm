Where can I get information about the marketing of ABSOLUT VODKA?

If you send us your post address, we can mail you information about the advertising and marketing of ABSOLUT and how ABSOLUT VODKA, originally introduced in Sweden in 1879, was launched on the world market in 1979.

Since we have to be sure we do not send the information to anyone under the legal drinking age, please send a photocopy of your driver's licence or other ID along with your name and address to:
ABSOLUT PANUSHKA, The Absolut Company, SE-117 97 STOCKHOLM, Sweden.

How many ABSOLUT VODKA ads are there?

There are today about 500 ads in the more than 17 year old and still evolving campaign, all built on the same very well-known concept.

In the US, a book about the history of ABSOLUT VODKA advertising was released in October 1996. You can find ABSOLUT BOOK at all leading book stores. If you want to know where to find the book outside of the US, please contact the publisher Journey Editions, an imprint of Charles E. Tuttle Co. Inc. at 153 Milk Street, 5th Floor, Boston, MA 02109 for further information.

Send a photocopy of your driver's licence or other ID to: ABSOLUT Advertising Reprints, The Jay Group, Department AV010, Ronks, PA 17573, USA.

Where do I send my ideas for new ABSOLUT ads?

Unfortunately, for copyright law reasons, we are unable to accept ideas from outside The Absolut Company and the advertising agency. But thank you for your enthusiasm.

Can you give me a few examples on some of the famous artists and fashion designers who have worked for ABSOLUT VODKA?

Over the years, ABSOLUT VODKA has co-operated not only with painters and fashion designers, but with artists in all fields, including sculptors, crystal glass designers, photographers and jewelry designers. The first artist to depict the bottle was Andy Warhol in 1985. Other artists who have worked with ABSOLUT VODKA are Keith Haring, Kenny Scharf, Edward Ruscha, Stephen Spouse, Julia Wachtel, Gervasio Gallardo, Oscar Mariné, Yrjö Edelmann, Helmut Newton, Holly Johnson, Agatha Ruiz de la Prada, Gianni Versace, John Galliano, Helmut Lang, Azzedine Alaia and many, many others.
